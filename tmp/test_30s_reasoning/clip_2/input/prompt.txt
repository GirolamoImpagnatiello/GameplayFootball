### PHASE 2: DENSE NARRATIVE REWRITE
After drafting the JSON, generate the final caption. This must be a dense, multi-sentence narrative derived STRICTLY from your scene analysis. Put this final output inside `<final_prompt>` XML tags.

- Length & Format: Write exactly ONE coherent paragraph consisting of several full sentences. Do not use bullet points or lists.

- Taboo Words: DO NOT refer to the video itself. Avoid phrases like "the video...", "the scene...", "the clip...", "the frame...".

- Tone: Use clear, neutral, objective language without opinions or value judgments. No inferred thoughts or hidden motivations.
- Faithfulness: Only describe what is actually visible in the provided frames. Do not hallucinate or invent details not present in the video.
